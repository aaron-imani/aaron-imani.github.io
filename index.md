## About Me

Hey! I'm a Ph.D. Candidate in Software Engineering at UC Irvine 🇺🇸, where I work with [Prof. Iftekhar Ahmed](https://www.linkedin.com/in/iftekharahmed1) in the [STAIRS Lab](https://stairs.ics.uci.edu). Prior to this, I completed my Master's in Software Engineering at the University of Calgary 🇨🇦.
