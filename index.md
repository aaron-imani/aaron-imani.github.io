## About Me

Hey! I'm a Ph.D. Candidate in Software Engineering at UC Irvine 🇺🇸, where I work with [Prof. Iftekhar Ahmed](https://www.linkedin.com/in/iftekharahmed1) in the [STAIRS Lab](https://stairs.ics.uci.edu). My research dives into understanding the underlying mechanisms of Large Language Models for code intelligence through the lens of mechanistic interpretability 🤖💭. Prior to this, I completed my Master's in Software Engineering at the University of Calgary 🇨🇦.

Curious about what I've been working on? Feel free to check out my [CV](assets/files/CV_Aaron_Imani.pdf) and my peer-reviewed publications [below](#publications).

## Research Interests

- **LLM-Based Agents for Software Engineering:** agentic memory, reusable agent skills, repository-level code generation
- **Mechanistic Interpretability of LLMs for Code:** internalized software engineering concepts and their causal influence on model behavior
- **Context for LLMs in Software Engineering:** prompt evolution in real-world repositories, context enhancement that lets smaller open-weight models rival proprietary ones
